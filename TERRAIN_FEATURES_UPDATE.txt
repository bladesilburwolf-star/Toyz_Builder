Toyz Builder terrain expansion update

Implemented:
- deeper/wider procedural ravines
- sharp cliff/terrace carving
- natural land-bridge generation across shallow/noisy water gaps
- deeper cave-mouth carving
- real underground cavern tube/chamber geometry using textured rock
- real vertical waterfall geometry using the water texture instead of a cube placeholder
- cavern generation is deterministic from world seed
- cavern cleanup/regeneration lifecycle
- rock cavern rendering is double-sided and tinted darker to read as interior rock

Cave note:
The current terrain representation remains a heightfield, so the cavern entrances are formed by terrain depressions and the enclosed cavern geometry is layered underneath them. Full arbitrary overhang/undercut terrain requires the later 3D terrain/volume streaming step.
